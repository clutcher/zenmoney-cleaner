# How I categorize

The categorizer and the verifier read this file on every run, and its rules win over their own idea of a category. Write only your own choices, the ones a stranger would get wrong: the agents already know that a pharmacy is health and a supermarket is groceries. Keep rules you are unsure the agents need in `policy-candidates.md`, which no agent reads, and move a line here once a run gets that case wrong.

- A category says what was bought, not who bought it: clothes for my partner go to Clothing, not to Partner.
- A trip uses the categories of what was bought there; Travel holds only flights and hotels.
- A cafeteria is Cafes & Restaurants whatever the amount, and bakeries count as cafes too.
